Headline: Higher yields and higher oil hit the expensive parts of the tape

The market spent the session de-risking as Treasury yields stayed elevated and oil pushed higher, a combination that weighed most heavily on duration-sensitive AI and semiconductor exposure. Macro context pointed to inflation worries tied to Middle East tensions and rising crude, while the economic calendar added mixed U.S. data with July industrial production expected at 0.4% after 0.1% prior and capacity utilization expected at 76.4% after 76.1% prior; investors also looked ahead to Fed minutes and Jackson Hole.

The surface matched the internals more than the headline might suggest. SPY finished at 767.42 for -0.68% versus the prior 772.67, while tech-heavy risk underperformed: QQQ and SMH intraday tapes both showed deeper downside pressure than the broad market. Energy and parts of Financials held up better, consistent with XLE finishing above its prior close while XLK and Industrials lagged in the sector snapshot.

Cross-asset action reinforced the message. VIX futures were around 18.18 for +0.70%, WTI crude traded near 84.09 for +0.42%, gold futures near 4400.95 for -1.63%, and crypto context showed Bitcoin above $64,000 and modestly positive on the day while most major tokens were softer. The 10-year Treasury yield snapshot was 4.72% on the latest available curve date, with the 30-year at 5.31%, keeping the rate backdrop restrictive for long-duration growth.

CLEAN OPTIONS-FLOW ANALYSIS

The clean single-stock tape was dominated by downside semis and index-adjacent tech protection, with one notable small-cap bullish outlier.

KLAC — the day's dominant downside print

KLAC stood out twice at the same timestamp, 14:03:01Z. The largest print was 11,500 of the 2026-11-20 224P at 42.00 for $48.30M with stock at 197.94. Volume was 11,500 against open interest of only 33, which strongly suggests a fresh position, though opening status is still an estimate until a full OI update confirms. A second KLAC 2026-09-18 224P print for 9,000 contracts at 29.70 totaled $26.73M with existing OI of 10,090; that one is more ambiguous and could be part of a hedge adjustment or roll.

WOLF — the cleanest upside outlier

WOLF was the biggest clean bullish outlier. At 13:52:12Z, 22,500 of the 2027-06-17 45C traded at 9.48 for $21.33M with the stock at 32.40. That creates a breakeven near 54.48. Volume 22,500 versus OI 24,329 is large but not definitive by itself; with earnings due 2026-08-19 postmarket, this looks like long-dated speculative upside or a post-earnings convexity bet rather than a simple short-term gamble.

TSLA and META — late-day mega-cap protection

TSLA saw a large near-dated protective or bearish print late in the day. At 18:42:31Z, 3,750 of the 2026-08-21 400P traded at 64.80 for $24.30M with the stock at 335.61. The strike sat deep above spot and delta was about -0.96, which makes this look more like stock protection, replacement, or a structured hedge than a plain directional bet.

META also drew notable late-day downside premium. At 18:43:06Z, 2,350 of the 2026-08-21 625P traded at 78.15 for $18.37M with stock at 546.42, and another nearby short-dated META put print totaled $17.57M. Given the very short tenor and high delta, these are better read as urgent protection or hedge maintenance than casual speculation.
