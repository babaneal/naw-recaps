SECTOR PERFORMANCE

Energy was the only clear winner and it had the commodity behind it — WTI +2.63%, Brent +2.04%. XLE finished +0.28% with call premium roughly 5.6x put premium, which is one of the cleanest price/flow alignments on the board.

Financials and industrials were the drag. XLF -0.92% with puts ~1.8x calls; XLI -1.23% with puts ~3.1x calls. When the ETF is down and the option skew is that lopsided the same way, there is no interpretation game to play — it is bearish positioning confirmed by price.

Tech is where it gets more interesting. XLK barely moved (-0.20%), but under the hood the bull/bear premium split was ~$11.3M / ~$7.8M while the raw put/call split was ~$12.4M / ~$7.6M. Read together: money was selective, not broadly hedging out.

Flow vs price divergences worth flagging

XLC is the standout — down -0.57% on the tape but bullish premium $383K dwarfed bearish $92K (~4x). XLK is a softer version of the same pattern. Both are on the watchlist for follow-through tomorrow.

CLEAN OPTIONS-FLOW ANALYSIS

Loudest downside expression: MU near-dated puts

MU 1200P 2026-08-21 — 3,515 contracts at $250.40 for $88.02M premium, against only 256 open interest. Vol/OI ~13.7 is the tell. Underlying was ~$948.73 at the print and closed $974.05 after touching $929.07 intraday. Strikes so far above spot on the front-week are consistent with urgent protection or a synthetic short, not casual bearish speculation.

MU 1190P 2026-08-21 — two prints, same strike/expiry, minutes apart: 7,105 @ $240.75 ($171.05M) and 5,000 @ $221.19 ($110.60M). Total contract volume 19,968 vs OI 11,307. Repeated intraday hits in the same contract are what pushes this from 'one desk trade' toward 'coordinated activity'.

Loudest upside expression: AMD long-dated calls

AMD 430C 2026-11-20 — 11,500 contracts at $75.90 for $87.29M premium against just 289 OI. Vol/OI ~39.8, ask-side. The tenor, size and OI ratio together give this trade the cleanest bullish read of the session — with the standard caveat that ask-side alone is not proof of buying.
