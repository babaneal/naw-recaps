The cleanest conviction came from contracts where volume pressed against smaller prior open-interest bases or where open-interest change data confirmed fresh build, while many of the absolute largest premium prints were deep in the money, cross-traded, or floor-linked.

Most constructive clean signal

MSFT November upside remained aggressive. The 2026-11-20 480C print for about 39.1M executed ask-side with 7,250 contracts against 21,709 open interest. That is not rare on an OI basis, but it is still large enough to matter, and it landed in a session where MSFT stock closed 513.67 after opening 505.59.

The stronger structural build came from MSFT 2026-09-04 510C. Open-interest data showed current OI 5,729 versus prior OI context 1,414, an OI change of roughly +305.16% with 4,979 current volume. That points to likely new positioning rather than a simple unwind.

More informative was the shorter-dated 2026-08-28 1530C, where the data showed 5,764 current volume and a jump in OI versus prior 247, with 4 consecutive days of volume greater than open interest.

Given MSTR stock closed 127.30 after opening 134.01, that looks more like traders leaning into volatility and rebound optionality than a stock-confirmed directional winner on the day.
