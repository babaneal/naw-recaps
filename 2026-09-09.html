ANOMALY BOARD — TOP QUALIFIED FINDINGS

The strongest qualified anomaly in the reviewed sample was COIN 2026-11-20 170C. The aggregated alert showed 10,090 contracts, +$27.65M premium, all opening trades = true, prior OI 498, and ask-side premium concentration. The linked tape print at 16:02:07 ET traded 10,090 calls at 27.40 versus contemporaneous 26.80 x 27.95, putting quote location near 0.52. That is not aggressive enough for a stand-alone high-conviction ask-lift classification, but the full alert cluster was entirely ask-side and volume dwarfed OI, so the execution-side evidence supports a likely buyer-initiated call program with medium confidence. What makes it unusual is the size versus prior OI and the concentration in one contract. Alternative explanation: the alert itself also flagged multi-leg presence, so some of the premium may sit inside a broader structure.

PLTR 2026-09-18 135C was even larger in gross premium at +$40.50M for 11,266 contracts at 14:47:41 ET. The execution printed 35.95 against 35.70 x 36.10, a quote location near 0.63. That is technically ambiguous under the requested rubric, but every sampled tag on the print was ask-side and bullish, and the contract sat deep ITM with high delta around 0.98. That makes likely call buying the best fit, with medium execution confidence and low-to-medium opening confidence because prior OI was 21,572 and the trade did not exceed OI. Structural caveat: deep-ITM calls can substitute for stock or hedge stock, so portfolio direction is less clean than for OTM speculation.

META 2026-10-16 655C produced two distinct alert clusters after the close-equivalent window in the sample. One cluster showed 1,938 contracts and +$6.40M premium with ask-side concentration; a later cluster showed 1,648 contracts and +$5.52M premium with bid-heavy premium. Same strike, same expiry, same day, but opposite side signatures make the combined read unresolved. This is notable for repetition and strike concentration, but the side conflict lowers classification confidence.

MU stood out as event-driven volatility trading rather than clean directional conviction. A same-day-expiry 970C alert carried +$8.97M premium, 2,244 volume, and prior OI 378, but it was bid-side by sampled alert data. Together, the evidence points to elevated earnings-event volatility trading ahead of 2026-09-30 earnings rather than a simple bullish call-buying thesis.

AMD is the clearest example of why large premium alone is not enough. A 2026-09-18 470C single-leg trade at 15:45:12 ET printed 54.61 against 54.05 x 55.60, quote location about 0.36, with the sampled trade tagged bid-side and bearish. Later, a multi-leg call diagonal spread in 475/490 strikes printed with +$13.10M total premium but only +$387.8K net premium. That combination argues for mixed or structural flow, not a clean directional anomaly.
