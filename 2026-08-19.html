DEFINING THEME

SECTOR PERFORMANCE

Materials and communication services finished strongest, while technology, industrials and financials lagged. The interesting part is not the ranking but the mismatch: several sectors saw call-premium leadership despite negative price action, which says traders were still reaching for upside or relief even as the underlying ETF closed lower.

Technology showed the clearest price-versus-flow tension. XLK fell -1.48%, yet call premium exceeded put premium and bullish premium slightly topped bearish premium in the ETF data — a notable divergence rather than clean confirmation of the price move. Energy was similar but less dramatic: XLE slipped -0.16% even as call premium heavily outweighed put premium, though bearish premium still ran above bullish, suggesting activity on both sides rather than one-way conviction.

MRNA Jan 21 2028 220C printed 150 contracts for roughly +$630.8K with zero open interest at entry. Against a stock that closed 174.38 after an enormous event-driven repricing, the trade reads more like long-dated volatility or upside exposure management than a simple fade. Breakeven near 262.05.

CRDO Sep 25 230C traded 192 contracts for +$583.3K, volume 207 versus open interest of just 3 — a textbook volume-over-OI anomaly. The print leaned bid-side and came ahead of a 2026-09-01 earnings date. A real institution touched this line, but whether that was call selling, overwriting, or a larger structure anchor is unconfirmed without adjacent legs and next-session OI.
